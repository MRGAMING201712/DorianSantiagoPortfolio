Souls-like Arena Roguelite

Wave defense with Souls-style combat and roguelite progression. I prototyped in Blueprints first, then moved the whole gameplay stack into C++.

  • Wave spawner with difficulty scaling, boss waves and an endless mode
  • Combat built on a shared character base: combos with input buffering, soft lock-on through motion warping, weapon-trace hit detection
  • Enemy AI on State Tree with custom C++ tasks, plus an attack-token subsystem so only a few enemies swing at once
  • Attributes with derived stats and modifier stacks, and damage built from an FDamageSpec
  • Weapons with Souls-style stat scaling, upgrades and two-handing
  • Abilities (projectile, strike, heal, aura), XP and levels, souls currency, inventory and equipment

The bug that made a system

Players could attack and cast a spell on the same frame. Instead of patching each ability, I wrote an action state component that every action has to go through, so conflicting actions can't overlap.

Profiling before guessing

Big waves were dropping frames and Niagara looked guilty. A/B tests cleared it. dumpticks showed the real cost: enemy weapon meshes ticking, four components ticking every frame, and no update rate optimization on character meshes.

Respawn Game Jam · Fall 2026

A push-your-luck money game for two players on one computer. Spin the wheel to grow your pot, then bank it before you land on Bust, and play the stock market between spins. Every spin costs more than the last. After ten rounds, the richest player wins.

Made in Godot with a team of four for the Respawn Game Jam. I programmed the core gameplay:

  • The wheel and how it spins and slows to a stop
  • The luck logic that picks each wedge, including the odds of Bust
  • A stock market whose share prices rise and fall each round
  • Turn flow: free first spin, rising spin costs, banking, and the ten-round finish
