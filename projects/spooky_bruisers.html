Spooky Bruisers

You're the last human, and all you have are your fists. Fight off waves of demons, then spend what you earn on upgrades and new abilities.

What's in it

Wave spawner

A spawn manager runs the demon waves and starts the next one once the current wave is cleared.

Fists only

First-person melee. Punches lunge you forward, and hits land on animation notifies so the timing matches the swing.

Abilities and leveling

Clearing waves earns upgrades. You level up and unlock new abilities between rounds.

Radial ability wheel

Hold a key to open the wheel. Time slows while you pick, so choosing doesn't get you killed.
